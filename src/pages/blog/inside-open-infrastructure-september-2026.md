### OpenInfra's Job in the Open Source AI Stack

CNCF, OpenInfra and the PyTorch Foundation recently shared a stage in China for the first time, at KubeCon + CloudNativeCon + OpenInfra Summit + PyTorch Conference China 2026 in Shanghai. The unwieldy name turned out to be the point: a single inference request in production can touch GPU scheduling, KV cache, high-speed networking, vLLM and Kubernetes before it returns an answer.

The week produced a shared map of the open source AI stack. The PyTorch Foundation stewards the layer that trains and serves models. CNCF stewards the layer that operates and scales the workloads. Beneath both, *OpenInfra provides and controls compute, networking, storage and isolation on the hardware itself.*

Event sessions are now available on the [OpenInfra Foundation YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLYUhmBLBlro0). 

### OpenInfra Foundation News

* September brought major releases across the OpenInfra community, including OpenStack 2026.2 Hibiscus, Kata Containers 4.2.0 and Zuul 14.3.0. Next, contributors from across OpenStack, Kata Containers, StarlingX and Zuul will come together virtually for the [Project Teams Gathering (PTG](openinfra.org/ptg)), October 12–16, to collaborate across communities and shape what comes in future releases.  
* The OpenInfra Digital Sovereignty Working Group has kicked off a new series of discussions focused on sovereign AI. The group is bringing together participants from the Digital Sovereignty and OpenInfra AI communities to explore how open infrastructure can support greater control, choice and interoperability as governments and organizations develop sovereign AI strategies. [Join the sovereign AI discussion](https://etherpad.opendev.org/p/SovereignAI)!
